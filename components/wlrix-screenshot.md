# Screenshot

<code>wlrix-screenshot</code> &middot; Rust &middot; screenshots (region overlay, save and clipboard)

Freezes the screen, pulls out a region you can adjust, and saves it or copies it — after IRIX's own habits and KDE's Spectacle, drawn in 4Dwm chrome.

Reached by a key rather than by name. `wlrix-compositor` binds `Print` to drag a region out, and `Alt+Print` to grab the active window, frame and all.
